SiliconAperture needs a bigger window.

Photo, map and measurements sit side by side. Widen the window, or open it on a desktop.

How to use SiliconAperture
  1. Add a photo: drop, paste or browse, or try the demo.
  2. Pick the camera: EXIF fills it in; else a model, a preset or Unknown.
  3. Mark the object: click both of its ends on the photo.
  4. Give the distance: type it, or click camera then object on the map.
  5. Read the size. Solve for → Distance works it backwards and draws a ring of camera positions.
⚠ Accurate only when the object faces the camera. An angled surface is foreshortened, and the result is off.
